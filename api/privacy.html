🧠 BrainBridge — Privacy Policy

Français: BrainBridge accède uniquement à votre liste « BrainBridge Memory » dans Google Tasks (que l'application crée) et à votre adresse e-mail, pour vous identifier. Aucune autre donnée n'est lue.

1. What we access (Google API scopes)

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks — to read, create and delete notes only in the “BrainBridge Memory” task list that the app itself creates. We never touch your other task lists.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email + openid — to identify your account and issue your personal API key.

2. What we store

  • Your Google OAuth refresh token (encrypted at rest) — used to keep your memory synced.
  • Your BrainBridge API key — a random secret we generate for you.
  • Your email address — to link the key to your account.

We never store your password. We never read your email, Drive, Calendar or other Google data.

3. How you control it

  • Revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions → BrainBridge → Remove access. We can no longer reach your Tasks after that, and your refresh token becomes invalid.
  • Delete your notes by deleting the BrainBridge Memory list inside Google Tasks.

4. Data usage

  • Your notes are used only to answer your own questions via the BrainBridge API.
  • Not shared, not sold, not used for advertising.
  • When you enable an AI answer (GEMINI_API_KEY), the note context is sent to the model provider solely to produce your answer.
